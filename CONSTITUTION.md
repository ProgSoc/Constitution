## 1. Preamble

- 1.1. We, the members of **Programmers' Society**, in recognition of the importance of community, inclusivity, and student engagement, do hereby establish this Constitution as the guiding framework for our Club.

- 1.2. Our purpose is to create a welcoming and respectful environment where students at the University of Technology Sydney (UTS) can come together to share interests, build friendships, and develop leadership and professional skills. We are committed to upholding the values of **equity, diversity, safety, and respect**, and ensuring that all activities of the Club contribute positively to the UTS student experience.

- 1.3. This Constitution affirms our Club's **not-for-profit nature**, dedication to responsible governance, and commitment to working in partnership with **ActivateUTS** and the wider UTS community. It is established to ensure transparency, accountability, and sustainability, so that present and future members may benefit from the opportunities, connections, and experiences the Club provides.

## 3. Preliminaries

### 3.1. Name of the Club

- 3.1.1. The Club shall be named **Programmers' Society**, hereafter referred to as "the Club".

- 3.1.2. It may also be known as **"ProgSoc"**, where appropriate.

- 3.1.3. No other name shall be used or promoted unless specified in this section.

### 3.2. Affiliation

- 3.2.1. The Club is established and operates as an ActivateUTS affiliated club. Affiliation is conditional upon approval by ActivateUTS and compliance with the current ActivateUTS Club Affiliation Agreement.

- 3.2.2. Affiliation is granted for the period determined by ActivateUTS and is subject to annual reaffiliation. Affiliation does not automatically continue from one affiliation period to the next.

- 3.2.3. While affiliated, the Club, its Executives, and Members must comply with this Constitution, the current Club Affiliation Agreement and applicable ActivateUTS codes, policies, procedures and requirements notified or made available to the Club from time to time.

- 3.2.4. The President, Secretary, and Treasurer are authorised by the Members to enter into and renew the ActivateUTS Club Affiliation Agreement on behalf of the Club.

- 3.2.5. Affiliation does not create a partnership, employment, agency or joint venture relationship between the Club and ActivateUTS or UTS. The Club, its Executives and Members have no authority to bind ActivateUTS or UTS unless expressly authorised in writing.

- 3.2.6. Suspension, expiry, non-renewal and termination of affiliation are governed by the Club Affiliation Agreement and applicable ActivateUTS requirements.

### 3.3. Purpose and Objectives

- 3.3.1. The objectives of the Club are to:

    - 3.3.1.1. Promote the interests of UTS students in **computer programming and expertise in its associated disciplines, including software engineering, computer science, and information technology.**

    - 3.3.1.2. Provide opportunities for UTS students to engage in activities, events, and programs aligned with the Club's purpose.

    - 3.3.1.3. Foster an inclusive, respectful, and supportive environment for all members.

    - 3.3.1.4. Operate on a not-for-profit basis, with all income and assets used solely to further the Club's objectives.

    - 3.3.1.5. Support the mission and objectives of ActivateUTS through Club activities and governance.

## 4. Definitions

- 4.1. In this constitution, the following terms, unless otherwise stated, mean:

    - 4.1.1. "Affiliation agreement" refers to the ActivateUTS Club Affiliation Agreement.

    - 4.1.2. "Codes of Conduct" refers to the ActivateUTS Clubs Code of Conduct and the ActivateUTS Community Code of Conduct.

    - 4.1.3. "Student Complaints Policy" or "Complaints Policy" refers to the ActivateUTS Student Complaints Policy.

    - 4.1.4. "Club" or "Society" refers to **Programmers' Society** as established under this constitution.

    - 4.1.5. "Executives" refers to Executives of the Club which are elected by the members.

    - 4.1.6. "Financial Member" or "Member" refers to a member who has registered through ActivateUTS systems, unless a written exemption is provided by ActivateUTS, for the current calendar otherwise had it subsidised through an approved ActivateUTS partnership.

    - 4.1.7. "Staff" refers to a registered staff member of the University of Technology Sydney with a staff identification number.

    - 4.1.8. "Student" or "Students" refers to an enrolled student at the University of Technology Sydney, with a student identification number provided.

    - 4.1.9. "University" refers to the University of Technology Sydney, including UTS College.

    - 4.1.10. Monetary values and valuations are in Australian Currency.

## 5. Membership

### 5.1. Eligibility

- 5.1.1.  Membership is open to:

    - 5.1.1.1. All current students of the University of Technology Sydney (UTS).

    - 5.1.1.2. UTS staff, alumni, and members of the general public, provided that at least 66% of the Club's total membership comprises current UTS students.

    - 5.1.1.3. All members must register through ActivateUTS systems to be recognised as valid, unless a written exemption is provided by ActivateUTS.

    - 5.1.1.4. Additional eligibility criteria may be determined by the Executives, subject to ActivateUTS approval and only if they remain:

        - 5.1.1.4.1. Reasonable, transparent, and non-discriminatory.

        - 5.1.1.4.2. Consistent with the Club's purpose.

        - 5.1.1.4.3. Consistent with ActivateUTS and UTS requirements.

### 5.2. Categories and Fees

- 5.2.1. Membership categories and minimum fees shall be:

    - 5.2.1.1. UTS students (minimum $1)

    - 5.2.1.2. UTS staff (minimum $5)

    - 5.2.1.3. UTS alumni (minimum $10)

    - 5.2.1.4. General public (minimum $10)

- 5.2.2. UTS Student membership fees shall never be equal to or exceed the fees set for UTS Staff, Alumni, or public members.

- 5.2.3. All memberships expire on 31 December each year.

- 5.2.4. Renewals are due from 1 January of the following year.

- 5.2.5. The Executive may propose fee changes annually, subject to the ActivateUTS minimum fee threshold and approval.

    - 5.2.5.1. Any changes in fee must be approved by ActivateUTS, before being voted on by The Club's members during the AGM.

    - 5.2.5.2. No changes shall be made to membership fees at other times of year.

### 5.3. Rights and Responsibilities

- 5.3.1. All members shall have the right to:

    - 5.3.1.1. Attend, speak, and vote at general meetings.

    - 5.3.1.2. Nominate for and hold Executive positions, subject to eligibility.

    - 5.3.1.3. Participate in Club activities, programs, and events.

- 5.3.2. All members shall:

    - 5.3.2.1. Uphold the Constitution, Codes of Conduct, and policies of ActivateUTS.

    - 5.3.2.2. Act respectfully and inclusively toward other members.

    - 5.3.2.3. Support the Club's purpose and objectives.

### 5.4. Termination and disciplinary action

- 5.4.1. Membership may only be terminated under the following circumstances:

    - 5.4.1.1. Voluntary resignation submitted via email to the Secretary of The Club, or ActivateUTS.

    - 5.4.1.2. Breach of ActivateUTS Codes of Conduct or other relevant policies, as determined by ActivateUTS.

    - 5.4.1.3. Non-renewal of membership.

- 5.4.2. Termination does not entitle the member to a refund of fees.

- 5.4.3. The disciplining of club executives, members, and relevant UTS personnel will be investigated, handled, and processed by ActivateUTS in line with the ActivateUTS Codes of Conduct.

    - 5.4.3.1. Clubs are not to instate their own grievance or disciplinary policies, and should escalate all relevant concerns to ActivateUTS through the appropriate reporting channels.

## 6. Governance structure

### 6.1. Composition

- 6.1.1. The Club shall be governed by an Executive Committee consisting of:

    - 6.1.1.1. President

    - 6.1.1.2. Secretary

    - 6.1.1.3. Treasurer

- 6.1.2. Additional leadership roles may be proposed by the Club, including but not limited to:

    - 6.1.2.1. Vice President

    - 6.1.2.2. Events Executive(s):

        - 6.1.2.2.1. Events Coordinator

        - 6.1.2.2.2. Events Executive (Workshops)

        - 6.1.2.2.3. Events Executive (Social)

    - 6.1.2.3. Marketing Executive(s):

        - 6.1.2.3.1. Marketing Executive (Design)

        - 6.1.2.3.2. Marketing Executive (Social Media)

    - 6.1.2.4. Technical Executive(s):

        - 6.1.2.4.1. Technical Director

        - 6.1.2.4.2. Projects Executive

    - 6.1.2.5. Partnerships Executive

- 6.1.3. Any additional leadership roles and associated duties must be reflected in this constitution with ActivateUTS approval, prior to seeking majority approval at a valid General Meeting.

### 6.2. Eligibility of Executives

- 6.2.1. Executives must be current UTS students or staff members.

- 6.2.2. Executives must also be financial members of the Club at the time of nomination.

- 6.2.3. Other Executive eligibility criteria are not permitted without prior approval from ActivateUTS.

- 6.2.4. Executives shall support the mission and objectives of the Club and shall act in the best interest of Club Members, while representing ActivateUTS appropriately, and in accordance with Codes of Conduct.

- 6.2.5. No person may hold more than one position of President, Secretary and Treasurer.

- 6.2.6. No person may hold more than two positions simultaneously, and executives are discouraged from taking on more than one role at any given time.

- 6.2.7. Job-sharing of executive positions within a club (e.g. more than one Vice President) is not permitted without prior approval from ActivateUTS. Clubs must stipulate in this constitution if there are multiple positions within one portfolio and must provide differentiated responsibilities for similar roles.

    - 6.2.7.1. Co-Presidency is not permitted without prior approval from ActivateUTS.

### 6.3. General Duties of the Executive committee

- 6.3.1. The Executive Committee shall:

    - 6.3.1.1. Convene and attend meetings.

    - 6.3.1.2. Manage Club finances and operations.

    - 6.3.1.3. Ensure compliance with ActivateUTS policies, Codes of Conduct, and relevant legislation.

    - 6.3.1.4. Act in the best interests of the Club, and for a proper purpose.

    - 6.3.1.5. Executives must disclose any personal or financial interest in a matter before the Committee and abstain from the related decision.

    - 6.3.1.6. Take reasonable steps within their role and authority to promote a physically and psychologically safe Club environment.

    - 6.3.1.7. Comply with applicable ActivateUTS and UTS requirements relating to health and safety, psychosocial hazards, discrimination, bullying, harassment, sexual harassment, gender-based violence, sexual misconduct, violence, victimisation and other harmful behaviour.

    - 6.3.1.8. Promptly report to ActivateUTS, through the prescribed reporting process, any serious safety incident, material psychosocial risk, allegation or disclosure of serious misconduct, sexual harassment or gender-based violence, significant financial irregularity or other material risk relating to the Club.

    - 6.3.1.9. Not independently investigate allegations of serious misconduct, sexual harassment or gender-based violence unless authorised by ActivateUTS.

    - 6.3.1.10. Complete all mandatory training required by ActivateUTS within the specified timeframe.

### 6.4. Election of Executives

- 6.4.1. Elections may only be held at an Annual or Special General Meeting, subject to ActivateUTS general meeting pre-approval processes.

- 6.4.2. A call for nominations for Executive positions must be issued via email to all eligible members at least fourteen (14) days prior to the Annual or Special General Meeting.

    - 6.4.2.1. Nominees must be financial members of the club to submit their nomination.

    - 6.4.2.2. Where only one eligible nomination is received for a position, the nominee shall be declared elected unopposed.

    - 6.4.2.3. Where no eligible nominations are received for a position, the position may remain vacant or be filled in accordance with the vacancy provisions of this Constitution.

- 6.4.3. An existing executive, who is not a candidate for any position requiring election, must be appointed to oversee the nomination process, conduct the election, count votes, and declare the results.

    - 6.4.3.1. Voting shall be conducted by secret ballot unless otherwise agreed and approved by ActivateUTS prior to the general meeting.

    - 6.4.3.2. In the event of a tied election result, a fresh ballot shall be conducted between the tied candidates.

    - 6.4.3.3. If the fresh ballot also results in a tie, the successful candidate shall be determined by lot conducted by the existing executive, who is not a candidate for any position requiring election.

    - 6.4.3.4. The Chair shall not exercise a casting vote in any election for Executive positions.

- 6.4.4. Election results and associated meeting minutes must be reported to ActivateUTS within fourteen (14) days, via the appropriate governance submission avenues.

### 6.5. Executive terms

- 6.5.1. Each Executive shall serve a one-year term.

    - 6.5.1.1. The term shall begin at the AGM and conclude at the next AGM, in line with the ActivateUTS Reaffiliation period (October -- November annually).

    - 6.5.1.2. Executives agree to provide guidance in the handover of club operations until handover is complete or by 31st of December of that year.

    - 6.5.1.3. Executives may be re-elected for consecutive terms.

### 6.6. Vacancies of Executive positions

- 6.6.1. In the case of a vacancy, the Executive Committee may appoint a
    temporary replacement to carry out relevant duties until an SGM can
    be held, with a maximum term of 30 days.

- 6.6.2. The appointment must be approved by majority vote of the remaining
    Executives at an official executive meeting.

- 6.6.3. For mandatory executive positions outlined in Clause 6.1.1, a
    permanent replacement must be elected at an SGM within 30 days of
    vacancy, following ActivateUTS processes.

- 6.6.4. If additional executive positions cannot be filled after the 30-day
    period, they may remain vacant at the discretion of the Executive
    committee and be filled at a later point via an SGM.

### 6.7. Termination

- 6.7.1. An Executive's position will be terminated if they cease to be an enrolled and active UTS student or staff member.

- 6.7.2. An Executive may resign at any time by following the appropriate ActivateUTS processes.

- 6.7.3. ActivateUTS may terminate an Executive's position at any time for breach of policy or governance failure.

- 6.7.4. Executive positions may not be terminated without the knowledge of ActivateUTS, including at general or executive meetings, or by a majority vote from club members.

### 6.8.  Sub-Committees

- 6.8.1. The Executive Committee may establish a sub-committee consisting of members who assist with the day-to-day operations of the Club.

- 6.8.2. The sub-committee may include any number of specified roles, including but not limited to:

    - 6.8.2.1. Events Teams

    - 6.8.2.2. Marketing Team

    - 6.8.2.3. Project Team

- 6.8.3. All Sub-Committee members must:

    - 6.8.3.1. Be current financial members of the Club at the time of application or nomination.

    - 6.8.3.2. Ensure compliance with ActivateUTS policies, Codes of Conduct, and relevant legislation.

    - 6.8.3.3. Act in the best interests of the Club, and for a proper purpose.

    - 6.8.3.4. Be appointed by the Executive Committee through either:

        - 6.8.3.4.1. A majority vote at a committee meeting.

        - 6.8.3.4.2. An expression-of-interest or internal application process.

    - 6.8.3.5. Where roles are appointed by application rather than elected:

        - 6.8.3.5.1. The selection process must be transparent.

        - 6.8.3.5.2. Selection criteria must be clearly communicated to members, who may then express interest in applying.

        - 6.8.3.5.3. The appointment must be recorded in meeting minutes.

- 6.8.4. Sub-committee members support the Executive Committee but do not hold formal executive authority or have access to ActivateUTS platforms and resources unless they are elected to an Executive position.

- 6.8.5. All sub-committee members must report to a member of the official Executive team, and there shall not be any sub-committees for portfolios which do not exist within the Executive structure as outlined in this constitution.

- 6.8.6. Sub-committee membership shall not be a path to future Executive positions, nor shall it form any eligibility criteria for future Executive elections.

- 6.8.7. If a sub-committee member wishes to resign, they must make it known to the executive team.

    - 6.8.7.1. The executive team may fill the position through the regular appointment process for sub-committee roles, and do not need to inform ActivateUTS.

    - 6.8.7.2. If there are misconduct concerns regarding sub-committee members, the executive should raise the issue with ActivateUTS via email immediately, and await further instruction.

## 7. Executive roles and responsibilities

### 7.1. President's Duties

- 7.1.1.  The President shall:

    - 7.1.1.1. Oversee and coordinate Club activities.

    - 7.1.1.2. Ensure duties are delegated appropriately.

    - 7.1.1.3. Provide strategic leadership and guidance.

    - 7.1.1.4. Chair all Club meetings when present.

        - 7.1.1.4.1. In the President's absence the Vice president chairs, failing that the meeting elects a chair from the executives present.

    - 7.1.1.5. Call Executive and Special General Meetings as needed.

    - 7.1.1.6. Maintain knowledge of the Constitution and ActivateUTS policies.

    - 7.1.1.7. Represent the Club to external stakeholders.

    - 7.1.1.8. Prepare and present the President's Report at the AGM.

    - 7.1.1.9. Ensure the Treasurer submits a Financial Report at the AGM.

    - 7.1.1.10. Oversee financial record-keeping for re-affiliation.

    - 7.1.1.11. Support succession planning for future leadership.

    - 7.1.1.12. Hold signing authority for Club documents.

    - 7.1.1.13. Exercise casting vote in the event of a tie, except in the case of Executive elections.

### 7.2. Secretary's Duties

- 7.2.1.  The Secretary shall:

    - 7.2.1.1. Record and file minutes of all meetings.

    - 7.2.1.2. Document event proposals, logistics, and outcomes.

    - 7.2.1.3. Manage and handle membership records (name, student ID, contact details) for internal Club purposes only, and do so in accordance with the ActivateUTS Privacy Policy and Collection Notice.

    - 7.2.1.4. Present a Secretary's Report at the AGM, including membership count.

    - 7.2.1.5. Approve documents and agreements (with Executive consensus).

    - 7.2.1.6. Act as the primary point of contact for member concerns.

### 7.3. Treasurer's Duties

- 7.3.1. The Treasurer shall:

    - 7.3.1.1. Draft the annual budget.

    - 7.3.1.2. Facilitate financial decisions with the Executive Committee.

    - 7.3.1.3. Maintain accurate financial records and documentation.

    - 7.3.1.4. Present a current statement of accounts at each Executive meeting.

    - 7.3.1.5. Execute approved financial transactions.

    - 7.3.1.6. Enforce ethical use of Club funds and assets.

    - 7.3.1.7. Prepare a Financial Report for the AGM, including profit/loss and balance sheet.

    - 7.3.1.8. Co-manage the Club's bank account with the President.

    - 7.3.1.9. Provide financial documentation upon request by ActivateUTS.

    - 7.3.1.10. Submit the Annual Statement of Accounts and Balance Sheet for re-affiliation.

    - 7.3.1.11. Hold primary signing authority for Club finances.

### 7.4. Vice President Duties

- 7.4.1. The Vice President shall:

    - 7.4.1.1. Assist the President in their Duties.

    - 7.4.1.2. Act as President in their Absence, including chairing any club meetings.

    - 7.4.1.3. Oversee the internal operation and logistics of the club.

    - 7.4.1.4. Be the point of contact from external liaison.

    - 7.4.1.5. Ensure the Image of the club remains untainted to External Bodies.

    - 7.4.1.6. Instigate and maintain relationships with other ActivateUTS Societies.

    - 7.4.1.7. Maintain a positive relationship with ActivateUTS.

    - 7.4.1.8. Relay any information or concerns to the President.

    - 7.4.1.9. Attend meetings between External bodies and the club.

### 7.5. Events Executive(s) Duties

- 7.5.1. The Events executives shall:

    - 7.5.1.1. Be responsible for the organisation and scheduling of events for the society.

    - 7.5.1.2. Identify and manage event operational, risk and logistical requirements of events and activities.

    - 7.5.1.3. Drive the coordination of services and delivery of event aspects.

    - 7.5.1.4. Collaborate with other executives with regards to events requirements, objectives, budget, and event progress.

    - 7.5.1.5. Liaise with other executives with regards to the planning and promotion of events.

    - 7.5.1.6. Coordinate with the relevant stakeholders to ensure the event space is suitable and booked.

- 7.5.2. The differentiation between Events Executive(s) duties shall be
    as follows:

    - 7.5.2.1. The Events Coordinator shall have primary oversight of:

        - 7.5.2.1.1. The itinerary of Society events and accounting for delays and other unexpected changes.

        - 7.5.2.1.2. Non-technical resources and setups required for activities within the Society, including but not limited to catering and room layout arrangement.

        - 7.5.2.1.3. All ActivateUTS Event application, risk, and reporting requirements which shall be submitted within the required timeframes.

    - 7.5.2.2. The Events Executive (Workshops) shall have primary oversight of:

        - 7.5.2.2.1. Maintaining alignment between the topics of workshop events with the objectives of the Club.

        - 7.5.2.2.2. Ensuring attendees of the Society's workshop events positively benefit from the workshops by gaining expertise in the workshop's designated topics.

        - 7.5.2.2.3. The dissemination of workshop-related content to event attendees and Society members.

    - 7.5.2.3. The Events Executive (Social) shall have primary oversight of:

        - 7.5.2.3.1. The themes and activities of the Society's social
            events.

        - 7.5.2.3.2. Alignment of the activities within social events with
            the objectives of the Society.

        - 7.5.2.3.3. Ensuring attendees of social events positively benefit
            from social interaction with one another.

### 7.6. Marketing Executive(s) Duties:

- 7.6.1. The Marketing Executive(s) shall:

    - 7.6.1.1. Oversee the marketing of the Society's events and objectives.

    - 7.6.1.2. Be responsible for the creation of the promotional material for the society.

    - 7.6.1.3. Coordinate with the relevant stakeholders to identify and develop the best methods of promotion.

    - 7.6.1.4. Expand the society's membership and event reach.

    - 7.6.1.5. Develop and Implement brand and marketing strategy.

    - 7.6.1.6. Be aware of and adhere to ActivateUTS and/or UTS brand guidelines and policies.

- 7.6.2. The Marketing Executive (Design) shall have primary oversight of:

    - 7.6.2.1. The designing of promotional material pertaining to the Society, including but not limited to graphics, logos, motifs, and themes.

    - 7.6.2.2. The coordination of visual consistency across all the Society's promotional materials.

- 7.6.3. The Marketing Executive (Social Media) shall have primary oversight of:

    - 7.6.3.1. Management and moderation of the Society's social media platforms.

    - 7.6.3.2. Dissemination of all promotional material pertaining to the Society and its events and initiatives via the Society's social media platforms.

    - 7.6.3.3. The coordination of the timing strategies of promotional material dissemination on the society's social media platforms.

### 7.7. Technical Executive(s) Duties:

- 7.7.1. The Technical Executive(s) shall:

    - 7.7.1.1. Oversee the technical aspect of all Club activities.

    - 7.7.1.2. The Technical Director shall have primary oversight of:

        - 7.7.1.2.1. Any hardware required to host Club events and activities.

        - 7.7.1.2.2. The management, coordination, and contribution to the development of any software required to host activities within Society events.

        - 7.7.1.2.3. Monitoring of the curated hardware and developed software to ensure they work as intended when deployed during Society events.

    - 7.7.1.3. The Projects Executive shall have primary oversight of:

        - 7.7.1.3.1. The collaborative development of software repositories owned by the Society.

        - 7.7.1.3.2. The design of society-owned software applications and packages developed from society-owned software repositories.

        - 7.7.1.3.3. Ensuring all development outputs are in the interest of Society members, the software's users, and the general public.

        - 7.7.1.3.4. Ensuring all collaborative efforts in developing software under the Society's ownership remain respectful, constructive, and in the interests of all collaborators involved.

### 7.8. Partnerships Executive Duties:

- 7.8.1. The Partnerships Executive shall:

    - 7.8.1.1. Serve as the main point of contact with external industry connections and sponsors.

    - 7.8.1.2. Liaise with prospective and existing external connections and sponsors to ensure alignment between Society initiatives and external sponsorship requirements.

    - 7.8.1.3. Manage and reach agreements with external sponsors regarding the provision of financial support and merchandise from sponsors to the Society.

    - 7.8.1.4. Be responsible for all ActivateUTS Sponsorship agreements, applications, invoicing, and reporting requirements within required timeframes.

## 8. Meetings

### 8.1. General Meetings

- 8.1.1. The Club shall conduct an Annual General Meeting (AGM) each calendar year, as specified by ActivateUTS. It may also hold Special General Meetings (SGMs) and Executive Committee Meetings as required.

- 8.1.2. Notice must be given by way of email to all members at least fourteen (14) days in advance for all General Meetings.

    - 8.1.2.1. This notice must include all relevant details, such as date, time, and agenda, as required by ActivateUTS General Meeting and Governance Procedures.

- 8.1.3. Approved constitutional amendments must be circulated to all members via email, at least seven (7) days in advance for all General Meetings, given the fourteen (14) day minimum announcement requirement has already been satisfied.

- 8.1.4. The Annual General Meeting (AGM) is a compulsory meeting held once per year to:

    - 8.1.4.1. Review of the Club's activities and performance

    - 8.1.4.2. Elect Executives for the upcoming term

    - 8.1.4.3. Present annual reports and financial statements

    - 8.1.4.4. Propose and vote on constitutional amendments

- 8.1.5. Special General Meetings (SGM) are convened as required by The Club to address urgent or significant matters outside of the AGM period.

    - 8.1.5.1. SGMs may be called for ad hoc Executive elections, adoption of approved Constitutional amendments, or other major changes to The Club's governance or operations.

    - 8.1.5.2. SGMs may be convened by the Executive Committee, requested by ActivateUTS or by a written email request of at least ten (10) financial members to the Club Executives.

    - 8.1.5.3. The Executives must convene the SGM within 28 days of a valid request, failing which ActivateUTS may restrict club activities temporarily, until the governance requirements are fulfilled.

- 8.1.6. Quorum

    - 8.1.6.1. For a valid general meeting to occur, at least six (6) financial members of the club must be present to make the proceedings of that meeting valid.

    - 8.1.6.2. If within half an hour after the appointed time for the commencement of a General Meeting a quorum is not present, the meeting:

        - 8.1.6.2.1. Is to stand adjourned to the same day in the following week at the same time and at the same place, unless otherwise specified prior to the meeting.

        - 8.1.6.2.2. If at the adjourned meeting a quorum is not present within half an hour after the time appointed for the commencement of the meeting, the members present, being at least three (3), are to constitute a quorum.

        - 8.1.6.2.3. Special business, including constitutional amendments and dissolution, may not be decided with a reduced quorum.

- 8.1.7. Voting

    - 8.1.7.1. Each financial member shall have one (1) vote

    - 8.1.7.2. In an online or hybrid meeting, voting can be permitted electronically, with online attendance counting towards quorum.

    - 8.1.7.3. Proxies are not permitted for any General Meeting matters.

    - 8.1.7.4. Decisions shall be made by simple majority unless otherwise specified.

    - 8.1.7.5. The Chair shall hold a casting vote in the event of a tie, except in the case of an Executive Position Election.

- 8.1.8. For the General meeting to remain valid, minutes must be distributed to all Executives for review within seven (7) days and uploaded to relevant record-keeping locations provided by ActivateUTS within fourteen (14) days.

### 8.2. Executive Meetings

- 8.2.1. An Executive Meeting is to occur at least quarterly and can take place in person or via online conference.

- 8.2.2. The quorum for Executive Meetings is **50%** of the total Executive team.

    - 8.2.2.1. This may not include non-elected Club representatives who are not officially Executives, including additional leadership roles, or sub-committee members.

- 8.2.3. The Executive Meeting agenda must include, as a minimum:

    - 8.2.3.1. Acknowledgement of Country

    - 8.2.3.2. Review of previous meeting minutes, including action item updates

    - 8.2.3.3. Finance update from Treasurer

    - 8.2.3.4. Review of any activity or events from the previous month

    - 8.2.3.5. Plans for upcoming months

    - 8.2.3.6. General updates from each Executive

    - 8.2.3.7. Any motions to be voted on

    - 8.2.3.8. Any other business

- 8.2.4. Executive meetings may be called by either the Secretary, the President or jointly by Executive members

    - 8.2.4.1. The Executive member(s) calling the meeting must provide notice to all other Executive members in advance, at a time that is reasonably accessible to and agreed upon by all Executives.

    - 8.2.4.2. Executive Members may request that items of business be included on the agenda by submitting such requests to the Secretary before the appointed meeting time.

    - 8.2.4.3. The Secretary shall create and distribute the meeting agenda to the Executive prior to each meeting.

- 8.2.5. The President is entitled to chair all meetings. If the President is not present, the Vice-President is entitled to chair. If neither the President nor the Vice-President is present, the meeting must elect another member to chair.

- 8.2.6. A motion or resolution is considered passed if over 50% of Executives agree.

    1.  Outside of Executive Meetings, the Executive may make decisions by circular resolution. A circular resolution is valid if approved in writing, including by electronic communication, by at least the number of Executives required to constitute a quorum for an Executive Meeting.

- 8.2.7. For the Executive meeting to remain valid, minutes must be distributed to all Executives for review within seven (7) days and uploaded to relevant record-keeping locations provided by ActivateUTS within fourteen (14) days.

## 9. Finance

### 9.1. Banking

- 9.1.1. The Club shall hold a bank account in its name, as required by ActivateUTS.

- 9.1.2. All funds must be used solely for the objectives of the Club.

### 9.2. Expenditure and Authorisation

- 9.2.1. All payments must be authorised by at least two (2) Executive members, one of whom must be the Treasurer or President.

    - 9.2.1.1. ActivateUTS may also authorise transactions.

- 9.2.2. The President and Treasurer shall be the two primary bank signatories.

    - 9.2.2.1. A delegate of the CEO, or an ActivateUTS Clubs Manager or Coordinator shall be the third primary signatory.

- 9.2.3. Other Executives, who meet the training requirements for banking authority, shall be secondary signatories, who may co-authorise transactions with a primary signatory.

    - 9.2.3.1. Two secondary signatories alone may not authorise transactions.

### 9.3. Trustees and Property

- 9.3.1. The President and Treasurer shall act as ex officio Trustees of the Club.

- 9.3.2. Trustees may execute documents and transactions as directed by the Executive Committee.

- 9.3.3. The ActivateUTS Clubs Manager may act in place of a Trustee if required.

### 9.4. Account Management

- 9.4.1. The Trustees shall open and maintain a Club bank account at a branch approved by ActivateUTS.

- 9.4.2. Accounts shall be open for inspection by ActivateUTS or any member upon fourteen (14) days' written notice to the Treasurer.

- 9.4.3. Monies shall not be drawn except by orders signed by two approved Executives or approved ActivateUTS signatories.

- 9.4.4. All property and income of the club shall be applied solely to the promotion of the Club's objectives and in the interest of its members.

## 10. Constitutional Amendments

- 10.1. Notice of proposed constitutional amendments must be submitted to ActivateUTS for review prior to calling a general meeting.

- 10.2. Notice of ActivateUTS approved amendments must be given to all members via email at least seven (7) days prior to the general meeting given the fourteen (14) day minimum announcement requirement has already been satisfied.

    - 10.2.1. The Constitution may then be amended by a two-thirds majority vote at a valid General Meeting.

- 10.3. Amended constitutions and associated meeting minutes must be submitted to ActivateUTS within 14 days of the meeting date, via the appropriate governance submission avenues, or the changes may be invalidated and therefore not incorporated into the Club's governance.

## 11. Dissolution

- 11.1. The Club may be dissolved by a three-quarters majority vote at a valid General Meeting.

- 11.2. Written email notice of intent to dissolve The Club must be given to ActivateUTS and all members by the Executives at least thirty (30) days in advance of the planned General Meeting.

- 11.3. Upon dissolution, all funds and property shall be vested in ActivateUTS, which will wind up the Club's affairs.

- 11.4. Distribution of Assets 

    - 11.4.1. Upon dissolution, all assets and funds must be transferred to ActivateUTS or another non-profit body nominated by ActivateUTS.

    - 11.4.2. No assets may be distributed to members upon dissolution.

## 12. Implementation

- 12.1. This Constitution is the default governing document for all ActivateUTS affiliated Clubs.

- 12.2. Where a topic is not covered, the Club shall default to the ActivateUTS Codes of Conduct, ActivateUTS Privacy Policy, ActivateUTS Student Complaints Policy, and other relevant ActivateUTS processes and requirements.

- 12.3. This Constitution shall be binding upon approval by ActivateUTS and successful adoption at a valid General Meeting.

- 12.4. In the event of an inconsistency between this Constitution and the internal club processes, this Constitution prevails to the extent of the inconsistency.

- 12.5. The Executive Committee shall review and seek approval for any changes to the Constitution annually prior to the AGM.
